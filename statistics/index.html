Stats

Where your time really goes: by day, week and month, for each activity, as streaks of days in a row, and for your sleep.

Open the stats with the chart button on the main screen. There are four sections at the top:

  • Overview — all your activities together: a day, a week, a month, and all your days in a row.
  • Activity — everything about one activity: a summary, a monthly calendar, and a year map.
  • Streaks — how many days in a row you've kept up each activity that has “Streak” turned on.
  • Sleep — how much and when you've slept over the past two weeks.

The app remembers the section you opened last. Tap a day in a week, a month, the list of days, an activity's calendar or your sleep to see that whole day — and press back to return to where you were. Everything is calculated on your phone — no internet, no sign-up.

When does a day start?

In Timespan, a day starts when you wake up — when “Sleep” ends — not at midnight. If you go to bed at 2 a.m., those two hours still belong to the evening before. If no sleep is logged, the new day starts at the time you choose (4:00 a.m. by default; change it in Settings under “New day starts at”).

The one exception is the day bars in Week and Month. They show calendar days, midnight to midnight, so you can see which hours things happened in.

Overview › Day

This is the main stats screen. Flip between days with the arrows or by swiping sideways.

  • The ring — how your day was split between activities, with the total tracked time in the middle.
  • Woke up at … — when the sleep that started this day ended.
  • Totals — every activity: how long, and what share of the day.
  • Timeline — what came after what, from when to when. If you ran a pomodoro during an activity, you'll see tomatoes and your focus time under it (“🍅🍅 · focus 50m”).

You can fix an entry right from the timeline: tap “⋮” next to it.

Overview › Week and Month

At the top are your totals for each activity over the week or month, longest first. Below them is one row per day: a bar from midnight to midnight with each activity in its place. You can see when you work, when you have lunch and when you go to bed.

Comparison arrows. Each total has an arrow showing how much more (▲, green) or less (▼, red) time it got than in the same stretch of last week or month. A week in progress is compared fairly — with the same part of the week before, up to the same day and hour: on Wednesday, Monday to Wednesday against Monday to Wednesday, not against a whole week. A line under the totals says which days you're being compared with (“Arrows compare with November 9–14”). The colors aren't a grade: more isn't always better.

  • Yellow “=” — less than 5 minutes' difference.
  • Blue “NEW” — you didn't do this activity at all last time.
  • No marks — the previous period was empty, or you started using the app partway through it.

Overview › All days

All your days in a row, starting from today. Each one shows its activity icons, the day's pomodoros and the total tracked time. Scroll through to remember how your week went. Tap a day to open it in full.

Activity › Summary

Pick an activity from the row of icons to see its whole history. Tap any of the four numbers (Total, Per active day, Per week, Active days) to see how it's worked out.

  • Streak — at the top, if the activity has “Streak” on: how many days in a row, since when, your best, and lights for the past two weeks. See Streaks below for how they're counted.
  • Total — all the time you've tracked since this activity was first logged.
  • Per active day — the average across the days you did it.
  • Per week — the total divided by the number of days since the first entry (today included), times 7.
  • Active days — how many days had this activity, out of all the days since it was first logged.
  • By day of the week — which days of the week you do it most, on average. Tap a bar to see its number.
  • By week — the last 12 weeks as bars, so you can see if it's going up or down. Tap a bar to see its number above it.
  • Time of day — 24 dots; the deeper the color, the more often this activity falls in that hour. Below them are your two busiest hours in a row.
  • Records — your longest session, busiest day, longest streak and first entry, with dates.

Activity › Month and Year

Month — a calendar with the days you did this activity filled in with its color: the longer, the brighter. Swipe to move between months.

Year — 12 month rows with a dot for each day. You can see right away when the activity became a habit and when it dropped off. At the bottom: how many days and how many hours in total. The dots are too small to tap — to open a day, go to Month.

Streaks

A streak is how many days in a row you've kept up an activity. Streaks only count for activities with “Streak” turned on — check the box in My activities or on the activity's card.

  • A day counts if you spent at least 10 minutes on the activity.
  • Today never breaks a streak: you have until bedtime to keep it going.
  • On milestone days (3, 7, 14, 30, 50, then every hundred) you'll get a cheer when you start the activity.

At the top there's a short “How it's counted” note. Below it, each activity with “Streak” on gets a row, in the same order as on the main screen: its icon, name and best streak, with 31 dots underneath for the past month — today is on the right. An orange dot means the day counted. Next to the dots is how many days the current streak has lasted, or “no streak” if it's been broken. If no activity has “Streak” on, you'll see “No streaks yet.”

Sleep

For your sleep to count, mark an activity as sleep in My activities (by default that's “Sleep”).

  • Average, Bedtime, Wake-up — your usual times over recent nights.
  • Schedule — the last 14 nights: a bar from bedtime to wake-up on a shared scale, and how long you slept. The lines running through all the rows mark your usual times.
  • Sleep goal — nights shorter than your goal are drawn paler, and the line underneath says how many nights you were well rested. Set your goal in Settings (General › Sleep goal, from half an hour to 24 hours; 7 by default). The morning “Well rested!” message uses the same goal.

Naps and sleep that's still going don't count toward your schedule. Tap a night to open its day.

Questions

Where does my data go?
Nowhere. Everything stays on your phone. You can save a copy yourself: Settings › Data › Save to file.
Why is my day longer than 24 hours?
A day ends when you wake up. If you forget to switch activities (say, “Sleep” runs for days), the day stretches. Fix the times in the day's timeline.
Why are there no comparison arrows?
They need a full previous period. If you started using the app this week or last week, they'll show up later.
Can I view the stats in landscape?
Yes, every screen works sideways.
