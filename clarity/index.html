How it works

Goal → tasks → review → talk

The four sections are linked, so you can always see why each task on your list matters.

01

Set a goal

Write down where you want to end up — over a lifetime, a quarter, a month or a week. The coach helps you make it specific.

02

Plan your day

Break the goal into steps. Your daily list shows which tasks move you forward and which are just routine.

03

Review the day

A couple of minutes in the evening: what went well, what didn’t, how much energy you had. Weeks and months get their own templates.

04

Talk it through

The AI coach looks at your reviews and goals together and helps you decide what to change tomorrow.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Tasks, habits, goals, reviews and the journal all work offline and are stored on your device. When you’re back online, Clarity syncs your changes automatically. You only need a connection for the coach and voice input.

How do I use Clarity on both my phone and my PC?

Sign in with the same Google account on both, and your data syncs on its own. On a PC, sign-in opens in your browser.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and on our server, so it can sync between devices. Photos and audio from your journal never leave your device. You can make a backup or export everything to CSV in Settings.

Who’s answering in the coach chat?

A language model (AI). It only sees what you open in the conversation yourself: a goal, a daily review or an entry. You can flag any reply as inappropriate.

Is there an iPhone version?

It’s in the works. As soon as Apple approves it, the App Store button on this page will go live.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings → Account. Or follow the steps on the account deletion page — the link is at the bottom of this site.
