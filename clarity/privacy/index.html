Privacy Policy

What data Clarity collects, why, who it’s shared with, and how to delete it.

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Clarity is an Android and Windows app that connects your goals, tasks, habits and reflections, with an AI coach to help. It is developed by Sergei Neznaev (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your entries are stored on your phone or computer and on our server, so they sync between your devices. You sign in with your Google account.
  • When you use the AI coach, your request and the entries it needs are sent to an AI model provider. They’re never used to train models.
  • Journal photos and audio stay on your phone.
  • Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics never include your entries, and you can turn both off in Settings.
  • We don’t sell your data and don’t show ads.

What we collect

  • Account: your Google account email, name and ID — when you sign in with Google.
  • What you write: tasks, goals, habits and check-ins, reflections and their summaries, notes, insights, journal entries and your conversations with the AI coach. Stored on our server so they sync between your devices.
  • Voice: when you dictate, the recording is sent to our server for transcription and deleted right away; only the transcribed text is kept.
  • Journal photos and audio stay on your phone and are never sent to the server.
  • Sign-in traces: each sign-in session stores the IP address and device model, so we can spot a sign-in from someone else’s device or a stolen sign-in key. These are erased after 30 days; the session itself lasts until you sign out.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Android only): where in the code the error happened, device model, Android version and a random installation ID. None of your entries. On by default; turn them off in Settings → “Send crash reports”.
  • Usage statistics: how long the app is open on each section (tasks, goals, journal…) and counts of actions such as “goal refinement started but not finished”, along with the platform (Android or Windows) and app version. No text, titles or content of your entries. Stored on our server under a random installation number that isn’t linked to your account, and never shared with third parties. On by default; turn it off in Settings → “Share usage statistics”. Turning it off deletes what has been collected, and only the fact that you turned it off is sent.
  • Purchases: your subscription status and a fingerprint of the Google Play purchase token, so the subscription works only on your account. Payment details are handled by Google; we never receive them.

Artificial intelligence

For the AI coach to reply, summarize your reflections or help shape a goal, your request and the entries it needs are sent to the model provider. Providers process them to generate the answer under their API terms and don’t use them to train models. AI answers can be inaccurate and are not medical or psychological advice.

If you report an AI answer, we keep the text of that answer and your reason, to improve filtering.

Who we share data with

Only the services the app needs to work:

  • Fly.io — server (Frankfurt, EU); Neon — database; Upstash — rate-limit counters;
  • Anthropic (the Claude model) — the AI coach and summaries; requests reach it through the OpenRouter gateway. Groq and OpenAI — speech recognition and text processing;
  • Google — sign-in, Google Play Billing, Firebase Crashlytics.

Some of these companies are based outside your country, including in the United States; they process data on our behalf and under their own data protection terms. We don’t sell your data and don’t show ads.

Permissions

The app only asks for what its features need:

  • Microphone — voice input and journal audio notes, only while you’re recording.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and starting after a reboot — so task and habit reminders arrive on time.
  • Vibration — for reminders.
  • Internet — sync, the AI coach and crash reports.

Journal photos are picked with the system photo picker: the app only sees the photo you choose and keeps a copy of it on your phone. On Windows, Google sign-in opens in your regular browser.

Storage, security and deletion

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, only your account can access your entries. On your device, entries are kept in the app’s private storage, and your sign-in key in the system’s secure storage.

Android cloud backup (Google Drive) is turned off for Clarity. When you move to a new phone with Android’s device-to-device transfer, your entries come along but your sign-in key doesn’t, so you’ll need to sign in again.

Backups and spreadsheet exports you create in Settings aren’t encrypted, so keep them somewhere only you can open.

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, except sign-in traces, which are erased after 30 days. To delete your account, go to Settings → “Delete account”, or email us at abertaslilya@gmail.com. What’s deleted right away and what may be kept longer is described on the account deletion page. Data on the device itself is removed with “Delete data and sign out” or when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, get a copy, and have it corrected or deleted. Export your data: Settings → “Export to spreadsheet” or a backup. Delete it: Settings → “Delete account”. For anything else, email us at abertaslilya@gmail.com. If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Clarity isn’t meant for children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect children’s data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting anything else, we’ll say so here first.

Contact

Questions about your data or this policy: abertaslilya@gmail.com. Developer: Sergei Neznaev.
